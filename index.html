A little machine.
A traceable kind of magic.

01 / EMBED

Give meaning a shape.

The open-weight MiniLM model turns your question and each passage into vectors. It runs locally with Transformers.js and ONNX WebAssembly.

02 / COMPARE

Find a nearby memory.

Cosine similarity ranks passages by semantic closeness. Searching “an unfinished goodbye” can find a letter that never uses those exact words.

03 / READ

Keep the source in view.

Each result is an exact passage with a title and character offsets. There is no generated answer, séance persona, or reconstructed author’s voice.

Privacy, downloads & limitations

Your queries and imported text stay in this tab. This app has no analytics or server API. Its static host receives ordinary page and asset requests, including the initial model download. A service worker caches the app and model for offline reuse when browser storage permits.

The bundled model is English-oriented. Its rankings can be wrong or unhelpful, especially for negation, uncommon names, long passages or other languages. We split text into overlapping passages; these are retrieval results, not factual verification.

The model and runtime are pinned and served alongside the app. You can inspect the source, checksums and licenses. Browser storage may be cleared or evicted; offline availability then requires another download.
